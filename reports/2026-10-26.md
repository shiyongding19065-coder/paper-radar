### 1. From Smell to Solution: Multi-agent LLMs Analyzing Cyclic Dependencies for LLM-Enabled Refactoring

- Authors: Henrik Brunvatne Olafsen, Adela Nedisan Videsjorden, Arda Göknil, Sagar Sen
- Published: 2026-10-24
- Source: openalex
- DOI: 10.1007/978-3-032-39205-3_11
- Link: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-39205-3_11
- PDF: https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/978-3-032-39205-3_11.pdf

Abstract Cyclic dependencies are a persistent architectural smell that undermines modularity, blurs ownership, and elevates maintenance risk. While Large Language Models (LLMs) provide refactoring abilities, existing tools struggle to address smells without intervention evidence. We introduce a multi-agent LLM framework for cyclic dependency analysis, which transforms static dependency evidence into structured, verifiable explanations of architectural entanglement. The approach constructs a structural dependency graph, detects cyclic regions via strongly connected components (SCC), extracts cycles as minimal witnesses of cyclicity, then produces grounded analyses. These are produced by a hierarchical team of LLM agents that describe the position, structure, and architectural impact of each cycle, clarifying why it exists and where it can be safely resolved. Evaluation on seven open-source Python projects shows that evidence-linked analyses can guide LLM-driven edits, reducing strongly connected components size and improving structural quality.
